# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring teams reviewing Rizqi Busthomi Wisono's QA experience and practical testing work.

## Product Purpose

A bilingual CV and portfolio that presents QA experience and project evidence clearly, with the portfolio available as a separate page.

## Positioning

Show the work and its evidence directly: cross-industry QA experience and payment testing at Bank Raya.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, hosted as a static site.
- Indonesian and English language controls, plus light and dark themes shared through local storage.
- The CV page links to `/portfolio/`; the portfolio page links back to the CV.
- The Bank Raya work includes payment API automation with pytest and Android automation with Robot Framework and Appium.
- A supplied positive Virtual Account end-to-end recording uses dummy data from a development server, as confirmed by Rizqi.

## Brand Commitments

Use formal, professional language in Indonesian and English. Preserve the existing CV page's restrained green palette and light/dark modes.

## Evidence on Hand

- `index.html`: CV, work history, skills, and contact links.
- `portfolio/bank-raya-va-flow.mp4`: user-supplied development-server recording, confirmed to use dummy data.

## Product Principles

- Keep professional claims tied to confirmed work.
- Keep Bank Raya case details general and omit internal systems or credentials.
- Show project evidence only when it exists; do not invent outcomes or metrics.
- Keep CV and portfolio pages distinct and easy to move between.
